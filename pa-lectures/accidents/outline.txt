Topic: Accidents in Perfumery (happy and otherwise)

I. Overdose Of Aldehydes in Chanel No. 5
	A. Creation of Chanel No. 5
		i.   Ernest Beaux/assistant
		ii.  1% aldehydes (C10-C12) added by mistake
	B. Coco Chanel's reaction
		i.   "Abstract" and "smells like a woman"
		ii.  Fizzy/bubbly, clean laundry, soapy, powdery
	C. Perfume history
		i.   Prior to 1921, perfumes were mostly floral
		ii.  No. 5 kicked off floral aldehyde fragrances in perfume
		iii. Many, many dups/copycats

II. Discovery Of The Scent Of Violets
	A. Search for irone
		i.   Orris root abs rather than violet abs
		ii.  Discovery of "iris ketone" (= irone) -
				but not (strong) violet smell
		iii. Sulfuric acid bath - strong violet smell :+1: => ionone
		iv.  Composition: a-ionone, b-ionone, and dihydro-b-ionone
	B. Ferdinand Tiemann
		i.   Coumarin
		ii.  Vanillin
		iii. Symrise
